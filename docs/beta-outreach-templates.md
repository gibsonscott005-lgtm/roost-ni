# Beta outreach templates — RoostNI

Use these for organic, low-friction recruitment in Northern Ireland landlord and tenant Facebook groups or community forums. Keep posts honest, short and local. Do not buy ads, bulk-message strangers, or invent user numbers.

**The 3-second rule (applies to every post):** the first line must say exactly what RoostNI is and who it's for, before Facebook's "See more" cut-off — value first, story later. Approved first line: *"RoostNI is a Northern Ireland renting platform for Northern Ireland — landlords list directly, tenants apply with one ready-made passport."* Never open with a question, a story, or "I'm a local developer" — that comes after the hook.

## Where to post

Aim for 3–5 groups that are clearly based in Northern Ireland and allow property or renting discussion. Good signals: local area names, landlord/tenant questions, or NI-specific rules.

- Local area/community Facebook groups (e.g. "Belfast Renters", "NI Landlords", county/town groups)
- University or graduate groups for tenant testers (Queen's, Ulster, Magee)
- Small-business or property-investor groups where self-managing landlords already ask compliance questions

## What we learned from the first week (use this)

Based on real traffic to the site:

- **Everyone is on their phone.** Keep posts to short lines and one link. No long paragraphs.
- **Evening is when people click.** Traffic peaked 9–10pm. Post between 7:30pm and 9:30pm, not in the morning.
- **People browse before they commit.** Listings and the audit get views; the sign-up form is where they leave. So never lead with "sign up" — lead with something they can look at with no account.
- **The audit works.** About 1 in 5 visitors who saw it completed it, in under 3 minutes. It's the strongest hook we have for landlords.
- **Don't bump quiet posts.** If a post gets no comments after a day, move on to the next group.
- **Real tenant demand is the landlord hook now.** Renters are signing up for passports and messaging about listings — lead with that when talking to landlords, not with features.
- **The Facebook page works on its own.** Traffic keeps arriving on days with no group post. Tag @Roostni in group posts so people can follow the page.
- **Keep question counts current.** The audit is now 14 questions (tenant vetting and agent fees were added) — never say "twelve".

## Follow-up cadence

1. Post in one group per day, between 7:30pm and 9:30pm.
2. After 48 hours, reply individually to anyone who comments or reacts.
3. If someone signs up, send a short welcome message with the help link and what to test first.
4. If a post sits with zero comments after a day, don't bump it or reply to yourself — move on to the next group.
5. After 7 days with no reply anywhere, post a short follow-up in the most responsive group.

## What not to do

- Don't claim anything or anyone is "verified" — applicant documents are self-certified, and RoostNI doesn't vet tenants.
- Don't promise features that aren't live (live identity provider, payments, safety certificate vault).
- Don't mention councils or "council oversight" — NI private tenancies are governed centrally (deposit scheme adjudication, Landlord Registration Scheme, Small Claims Court). Mainland-GB wording makes local landlords sceptical.
- Don't post in groups that ban promotion.
- Don't buy ads or pay for sign-ups.
- Don't invent testimonials, user counts or demand stats.

## Replying to property enquiries ("is this available?")

Anyone messaging about a listing is a real prospective tenant — the most valuable kind of visitor. Be upfront straight away: the listings currently on the site are beta examples, not genuine lets. Never imply a property is available when it isn't. Then turn their interest into feedback: invite them to try RoostNI free and tell you what's confusing or missing.

Full reply (use when they ask for details):

```text
Thanks for the message! Just to be upfront with you: that listing is a test property I'm using to trial a new renting platform I'm building for Northern Ireland — so it's not a genuine let, and I don't want you going through the process for nothing.

The site itself is live and free to try — core tools are free forever. It's built for renters: instead of re-sending ID, payslips and references over Messenger for every place, you fill in one passport and share it with landlords.

If you're currently looking, you'd be a brilliant person to try it and tell me what's confusing or missing (use test documents, not real ones):
https://roostni.co.uk/beta

Happy to answer any questions here too.
```

Shorter version (if they just asked "is this still available?"):

```text
Thanks for asking! Quick honesty: that's a test property I'm using to try out a new NI renting platform, not a real let. If you're house-hunting though, the site itself is free to try — core tools are free forever — one passport with your details instead of re-sending everything to every landlord: https://roostni.co.uk/beta
```

If they say "so there are no real properties?":

```text
Correct at the moment — it's early days and the listings are examples while I test the site with real landlords. The idea is that landlords list directly here and tenants apply with a ready-made passport, no agent fees. If you'd like to be first in line when real properties go up, you can set up a free passport now: https://roostni.co.uk/beta
```

Notes:

- Reply the same evening if you can — renting enquiries go cold fast.
- Do not promise when real listings will appear, or name landlords.
- This is a strong signal: real tenants are finding the listings. Consider it when deciding what to build or post next.
