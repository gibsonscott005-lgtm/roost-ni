# Resolve LANI approach decision points

## Context

These are the three unresolved decision points at the end of the approved LANI outreach plan.

## Decision 1: Sender address

**Recommendation:** Send from a founder address, not `hello@roostni.co.uk`.

**Why:** LANI is a small, relationship-driven membership body. A personal email from the founder lands warmer, signals accountability, and makes it easier to reply to a real person. A generic inbox is fine for inbound support, but the first contact to a partner body should feel direct. The signature can still include `hello@roostni.co.uk` as a fallback contact.

## Decision 2: Invite mechanism

**Recommendation:** Direct them to `/beta` first, then offer a LANI-specific invite code only if they ask for a private group.

**Why:** The `/beta` page already explains the beta, the role split, and the ground rules. Leading with a public beta link keeps the first email low-friction and lets LANI see the product without a special code. A bespoke invite code (e.g. `LANI-BETA-26`) can be offered as a follow-up if they want to refer a small group of members, which makes the offer feel tailored rather than presumptuous.

## Decision 3: Attachment format

**Recommendation:** Attach a one-page PDF, and also link to `/beta` in the body.

**Why:** PDFs are easy to forward inside an organisation and read offline. A short link to `/beta` in the email body gives them an immediate path to click through. The status report should be the attachment, not the link, because `/beta` is more of an onboarding page than a product summary.

## Proposed email update

- From: founder address
- Body: includes link to `https://roostni.co.uk/beta`
- Attachment: `roost-ni-lani-status-report.pdf`
- Follow-up: offer `LANI-BETA-26` code if they want to share with a small group

## One small change

Add a brief closing line to the draft email making the sender and offer explicit, e.g.:

> I am happy to arrange a 20-minute call, receive written feedback, or set up a small private beta group for LANI members if that would be useful.
