# Roost status report for a LANI approach

A one-off printable document (no app changes) summarising where the build is and
what must be finished before approaching the Landlords Association Northern Ireland.

## What the document contains

1. **What Roost is** — agent replacement for the small self-managing NI landlord;
   the "Roost assists, it never decides" boundary stated up front.
2. **Built today** — listings with NI fields (LRN, fuel policy, Fair Fuel),
   applications and passport sharing, self-certified tenant passport (identity,
   income with benefits-fair calculation, references), lease issue → tenant sign →
   landlord counter-sign, Tenancy Information Notice, deposit recording with the
   28-day countdown, inventory and utilities, rent schedule and ledger,
   maintenance tickets, notifications ledger with preferences.
3. **Identity verification** — the one claim Roost stands behind: provider
   document + face-match check, tenant-paid, currently running in simulated mode
   pending live provider keys.
4. **Deliberately not done** — no vetting, scoring, ranking or recommending; no
   credit/employment/background checks; no Right to Rent (England-only).
5. **Gaps before LANI**, in the agreed order: safety certificates vault,
   check-out and deposit return with disputes, notice to quit and rent increases,
   year-end tax summary. Plus: switch identity checks from simulated to live,
   and a short data-protection / terms note.
6. **Suggested ask of LANI** — what feedback would be most useful from them.

## Technical notes

- Generated with docx-js (US Letter, Arial), saved to `/mnt/documents/`.
- Content drawn from `docs/positioning.md`, the memory files and the current
  routes; no source files are modified.
- Delivered as .docx plus a PDF for printing.

## Format

Roughly 2 pages, British English, headings and bullets, no invented metrics or
social proof.
