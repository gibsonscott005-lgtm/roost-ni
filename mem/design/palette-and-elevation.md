## Brutalist Night (chosen 21 Oct 2026, replaces Warm Porcelain)

- Ground near-black ~#0F141C, crisp white headings, vivid emerald ~#00C88C (`--verified`).
- Emerald now used for accent containers, callouts and key data points (£0, 1mo, 28 days) as well as verification/success.
- Thin crisp border boxes, sharp geometry (radius 0.25rem), heavy display headings.
- Primary buttons are white with near-black text.

## Elevation tokens
Never hand-write shadows. Use `shadow-resting`, `shadow-raised`, `shadow-floating`, `shadow-verified` (hard offset, no blur) from `src/styles.css`.
