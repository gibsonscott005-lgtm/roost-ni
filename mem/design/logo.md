## RoostNI logo (chosen Sep 2026)

The brand logo is a solid charcoal near-black house with a clear pitched roof,
cradled in an emerald green nest/roost arc, with the wordmark "RoostNI" beneath
("Roost" charcoal, "NI" emerald). User rejected an earlier thin line-art version
as hard to read — always use bold, highly legible lettering for the brand.

Assets:
- `src/assets/roostni-logo-mark.png` — emblem only (house + nest arc), transparent; used via `src/components/logo.tsx` (`<LogoMark />`) in every page header.
- `public/favicon.png` — 64px emblem.
- `/mnt/documents/roostni-logo.png` — full master logo (emblem + wordmark).
- `/mnt/documents/roostni-facebook-avatar.png` — circle-safe square avatar for the Facebook page.

Rules: never reintroduce the generic lucide `Home` tile in headers; keep the
emerald arc — it is the brand accent, distinct from the in-app "verified" token.
