Before answering or building anything that touches tenancy rules, deposits, notices, certificates or compliance copy, verify the information against current Northern Ireland legislation — never English law.

Authoritative NI framework:
1. Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006
2. Private Tenancies Act (Northern Ireland) 2022 (notice periods commenced 5 May 2022: landlord 4/8/12 weeks; tenant 4 weeks, 12 after 10 years; deposit capped at 1 month's rent; cash rent receipts mandatory)
3. Tenancy Information Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2023 (TIN within 28 days, free; Notice of Variation within 28 days)
4. Tenancy Deposit Schemes Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2012 — approved schemes are TDS Northern Ireland and MyDeposits Northern Ireland ONLY; Letting Protection Service NI (LPS NI) closed 31 March 2020 and must never be listed
5. Landlord Registration Scheme Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2014 (£70 online, 3-year renewal)
6. Smoke, Heat and Carbon Monoxide Alarms for Private Tenancies Regulations (NI) 2024 (interlinked smoke alarms in living room + circulation spaces, heat alarm in kitchen, CO alarm near combustion appliances)
7. Rent Book Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2007

Strictly excluded (English law, never applies in NI): Section 21, Section 8, Assured Shorthold Tenancies, How to Rent booklet, Right to Rent, 5-week deposit cap.

Lease documents cite: "Private Tenancies (Northern Ireland) Order 2006 (as amended by the Private Tenancies Act (Northern Ireland) 2022)".

All legal copy carries a general-information/not-legal-advice disclaimer and references nidirect.gov.uk or the Department for Communities.
