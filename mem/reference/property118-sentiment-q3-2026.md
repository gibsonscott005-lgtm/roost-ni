# Property118 Landlord Sentiment Survey Q3 2026 (published 9 Oct 2026)

Source: https://www.property118.com/results-of-the-property118-landlord-sentiment-survey-q3-2026/
1,654 landlords surveyed 1–8 Oct 2026 (GB-heavy sample — never quote its percentages as describing NI landlords).

## Headline findings
- Confidence falling: ~75% of landlords lack confidence in the private rented sector outlook.
- Two thirds intend to sell or leave the sector, and are acting on it.
- Policy (tax, regulation, landlord/tenant rights balance) — not the market — is driving exit decisions.

## NI-relevant takeaways (reference for Robin/marketing)
- The regulation driving the exodus is English: Renters' Rights Act, Section 21 abolition, England's compliance stack. None of it applies in NI.
- NI landlords read GB landlord media and get frightened by laws that don't govern their tenancies — the same "UK law vs NI law" trap the SEO guides target.
- Counter-message opportunity: NI's framework (Private Tenancies (NI) Order 2006 + Private Tenancies Act (NI) 2022) is comparatively stable and landlord-workable; RoostNI makes NI compliance feel light.
- Framing rule: always present these findings as "what's happening across the water", never as NI sentiment.
